Flight Academy (aeronautics, fixed-wing planes, airfoil gliders & things that fly), and 3. STEM Academy (hands-on STEM + Art / STEAM maker projects that can include flight & drones without being focused exclusively on them).

CHOOSE YOUR ACADEMY FOCUS: DRONES, FLIGHT & AERONAUTICS, OR STEM + ART

Whether your school, after-school program, or camp wants pure micro-drone piloting, foundational aeronautics & planes that fly, or creative STEM + Art (STEAM) 3D-printed engineering projects, our three Academy divisions let educators tailor every workshop.

DIVISION 01 | MULTI-ROTOR & FPV FOCUS GRADES 3-12 & ADULTS

1. OSITO DRONE ACADEMY

Dedicated Focus: Drones, Quadcopters, FPV Piloting & Avionics

Our dedicated multi-rotor drone division focused 100% on unmanned quadcopters, FPV goggles, Betaflight stabilization, and enclosed obstacle course racing inside our modular floor-to-ceiling netted flight arenas.

  • Sub-250g Ducted Fleet: Fly our in-house 3D-printed CUB-75 (39.5g trainer) and SHARD-75 (45.5g optical-flow & bumper-drone platform).
  • 4-Tablet 3D Flight Sim Pod: Master Mode-2 stick control with Radiomaster Pocket ELRS controllers before live takeoff.
  • Drone Avionics & Telemetry: Learn gyroscope PID tuning, brushless motors, 1S LiPo safety, and FAA TRUST youth rules.
  • In-House 3D Printed Hardware: Ducted PETG-CF/PAHT-CF airframes, TPU 95A HF impact bumpers, canopies & sim stands (only motors, AIO flight boards, cameras & M1.4/M2 screws are non-printed).

DIVISION 02 | AERONAUTICS & PLANES FOCUS GRADES TK-8 & CAMPS

2. OSITO FLIGHT ACADEMY (DRONE ACADEMY FLIGHT)

Dedicated Focus: Aeronautics, Planes, Gliders & Things That Fly

Built for Exploring the science of everything that flies! Students investigate fixed-wing airplanes, camber airfoils, rubber-band & electric prop planes, ornithopters, and atmospheric flight physics from paper to 3D-printed wings.

  • The 4 Forces of Flight: Hands-on experiments testing Lift, Weight (Gravity), Thrust, and Drag plus Bernoulli's Principle and angle of attack.
  • Fixed-Wing Planes & Airfoil Gliders: Build and trim modular Aero-Cub K-5 3D-printed wing ribs, fuselages, elevators, and rudders (Pitch, Roll, and Yaw).
  • Wind-Tunnel & Launch Labs: Measure glide ratios, center-of-gravity (CG) balance, and propeller pitch thrust in classroom flight trials.
  • In-House 3D Printed Aircraft: 100% 3D-printed PLA+/PETG-CF snap-fit wings, stabilizers, propellers, and launch jigs (only steel nose-weight washers, music-wire axles, or micro-motors are metal/electronic).

DIVISION 03 | STEM + ART (STEAM) & 3D MAKER GRADES K-12 & MAKERS

3. OSITO STEM ACADEMY (STEM + ART / STEAM)

Dedicated Focus: STEM + Art Projects, 3D Design & Creative Engineering

Project-based STEM + Art (STEAM) workshops blending 3D CAD sculpture, kinetic mechanisms, illuminated LED art, robotics, and creative design. Projects can include flight and drone elements too, without being focused exclusively on them!

  • 3D CAD & Parametric Art: Design custom 3D models in our browser-based Osito 3D STL Editor, paint multi-color 3D prints, and build geometric gears & kinetic sculptures.
  • Creative Electronics + Art: Combine 3D-printed enclosures with LED light art, solar/battery circuits, magnetic wands, and optional flight/drone modules.
  • Storytelling & Coloring Workbooks: Pair hands-on maker builds with our 10 illustrated 28-page bilingual STEM + Art storybooks.
  • In-House 3D Printed Everything: We 3D print 100% of structural parts, gears, art frames, and jigs in-house on our Bambu Lab P2S farm—only metal fasteners/bearings/magnets and electronic circuits are non-printed.

SAN FRANCISCO IN-HOUSE ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING POLICY

EVERYTHING 3D-PRINTED IN-HOUSE (UNLESS IT'S METAL OBJECTS OR ELECTRONICS)

Across Drone Academy, Flight Academy, and STEM Academy, we design and manufacture our educational kits in-house on our San Francisco 2× Bambu Lab P2S Combo 3D print farm (`0.4 mm` hardened steel nozzles). By printing every airframe, wing airfoil, gear train, art sculpture, and classroom tool ourselves—and only sourcing metal hardware and electronics—we keep youth programs affordable, repairable on the spot, and customizable for every student.

3D-PRINTED IN-HOUSE (SF FARM)
  • Ducted drone frames (`PETG-CF` / `PAHT-CF`)
  • Fixed-wing gliders, ribs & props (`PLA+`)
  • STEM + Art sculptures, gears & stencils
  • Impact bumpers & canopies (`TPU 95A HF` / `68D`)
  • 4-Tablet Sim Pod stands & assembly jigs
NON-PRINTED (METAL & ELECTRONICS)
  • Metal Objects: M1.4/M2 steel screws, bearings, N52 magnets, steel axles
  • Electronics: Brushless motors, AIO ELRS flight boards, FPV cameras, LEDs, LiPo cells & tablets

THE OSITO LEARNING PATHWAY

Students don't just fly-they learn the foundational technology behind modern unmanned aerial systems, aeronautics, and 3D-printed STEM + Art builds:

STEP 1

1. ENGINEERING & BUILD

Understand lightweight drone components, 3D-printed ducted frames, brushed/brushless propulsion, and safe 1S battery management.

STEP 2

2. CONFIGURATION & CODE

Learn essential flight controller settings using Betaflight, gyroscope PID stabilization, and low-latency control protocols like ExpressLRS.

STEP 3

3. PILOT TRAINING

Master stick control and spatial awareness in our 4-Tablet Simulator Pod before taking the controls inside our netted pop-up flight tracks.

SAFETY FIRST, ALWAYS

Safety is our top priority for every school, camp, and pop-up event:

FULLY CONTAINED FLIGHT ARENAS All active flying takes place strictly within modular, reinforced floor-to-ceiling safety netting.
SAFE SUB-250G EQUIPMENT We fly lightweight, ducted 65mm micro-drones equipped with fully shielded propellers to prevent any impact damage.
PROTECTIVE GEAR & PROTOCOLS All students and participants are provided with protective eyewear and clear pre-flight briefings from our 3 certified instructors.
