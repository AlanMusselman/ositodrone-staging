THE 4-STATION DRONE FLIGHT LAB PROGRAM

No lectures and no sitting on the sidelines. Every student rotates through all 4 dedicated stations inside our enclosed Mission District studio—building real stick control, engineering skills, and FPV confidence step by step.

STATION 01 • LINE-OF-SIGHT FUNDAMENTALS

1. HOVER STATION

Master calm, precise takeoff, hover-in-place, yaw turns, and spot landings inside our enclosed floor-to-ceiling safety net using lightweight ducted micro-drones.

  • Mode-2 Stick Mastery: Throttle control, gentle pitch/roll, and box-pattern hovering.
  • Sub-250g Ducted Fleet: Fully shielded propellers designed for safe indoor youth training.
  • 1-on-1 Coach Spotting: Certified instructors coach every first takeoff and landing.
Hardware: CUB-75 / MiniCub65 Ducted Trainers • ExpressLRS RadioMaster Controllers
STATION 02 • 4-TABLET 3D SIM POD

2. 3D FLIGHT SIMULATOR STATION

Build real thumb muscle memory before touching a live battery. Pilots fly our custom San Francisco 3D Flight Simulator using real USB-C RadioMaster transmitters.

  • Zero-Crash Confidence: Practice throttle discipline, gate lines, and flips risk-free.
  • Real Radio Controllers: Exact same sticks and gimbals used in our live netted arena.
  • Practice at Home Too: Every student gets free browser access to our 3D SF Flight Sim.
4-Tablet Sim Pod • Real ELRS USB-C Sticks Launch Free 3D Sim →
STATION 03 • CRASH, BUILD, REPEAT

3. REPAIR & BUILD BENCH

Real pilots know how to fix their own aircraft. At our oak workbench, students learn how quadcopters actually work—swapping props, mounting 3D-printed parts, and running pre-flight checks.

  • Hands-On Pit Crew Skills: CW/CCW propeller direction, motor orientation, and frame swaps.
  • In-House 3D-Printed Spares: Frames, canopies, and TPU bumpers printed on our SF Bambu P2S farm.
  • Battery & Pre-Flight Safety: 1S LiPo care, fireproof charging habits, and failsafe testing.
Tools & Parts Included • Modular 3D-Printed Frames • Safe Low-Voltage 1S Bench
STATION 04 • 10X30 FT LED ARENA

4. FPV FREESTYLE & LED GATE ARENA

Put on First-Person View (FPV) goggles and fly directly from the drone's cockpit through glowing LED gates, suspended hoops, and freestyle dive lines in our 10×30 ft studio arena.

  • Live Cockpit View: Low-latency FPV goggles plus big spectator screens for parents & friends.
  • Glowing LED Course: Multi-bay race gates, elevated helipads, and freestyle obstacle lines.
  • Progression Badges: Advance from guided hover laps to timed FPV freestyle runs.
10×30 ft Coupled 3-Bay Netted Arena • GlowGate LED Course • Live Spectator Screens

STUDIO SAFETY & PARENT FAQ

Are the drones safe for beginners and young kids?

Yes. Every training drone is sub-250g with full 360-degree ducted propeller guards, flown exclusively inside our enclosed floor-to-ceiling safety netting with protective eyewear provided.

What ages can join the 4-Station Program & Birthdays?

Our 4-Station rotation is built for ages 7–17 (and adults for evening FPV sessions!). Younger pilots start on the 3D Simulator and assisted Hover cage before moving into FPV Freestyle.

Do we need to bring our own drones or controllers?

No—Osito Drone provides 100% of the fleet, RadioMaster ELRS controllers, FPV goggles, tablets, repair tools, and charged batteries. Signed parent/participant waivers are completed at check-in.

When does the 2141 Mission St, Suite 201 studio open?

Our target launch is January 5, 2027. You can lock in our 25% Off Founding Launch pricing today for January/February 4-Station cohorts and weekend VIP Birthday Parties.
